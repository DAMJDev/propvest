Wholesale Investor Certification

Required under s761G(7)(c) Corporations Act 2001 (Cth)

Why is this required?
Under the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth), only Wholesale Investors may access information memorandums and investment offers on this platform. To qualify, you must provide a certificate signed by a Qualified Accountant confirming you meet either the Net Assets Test (≥ $2.5M) or the Gross Income Test (≥ $250,000 p.a. for each of the last 2 financial years).

This certificate is valid for 2 years from the date your accountant signs it. After 2 years you will need to resubmit.

Your accountant must be a member of CPA Australia, Chartered Accountants Australia & New Zealand (CA ANZ), or the Institute of Public Accountants (IPA).
